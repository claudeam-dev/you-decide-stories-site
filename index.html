You Decide Stories

You Decide Stories is a YouTube channel that publishes short, serialized stories. Each day a new episode ends with a choice, and viewers vote by commenting "A" or "B". The most popular choice decides what happens next. Stories are written with AI assistance and narrated by an AI voice.

About this app

This page describes a small personal tool used by the channel owner. The tool uploads each finished episode to the owner's own YouTube channel as a private video, and reads the comments on the owner's own videos to count the A/B votes. It does not access any other person's YouTube account or data, and it has no public users.

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it, the owner agrees to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's data practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy.
